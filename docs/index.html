● PURE-RUST AUTOMATIC VIDEO/AUDIO SILENCE & MOTION CUT ENGINE

Sub-Millisecond S-Expression Cut Detection, Asymmetric Margin Padding & 8-Format NLE Export.

auto-editor-rs is a pure-Rust port of auto-editor that analyzes acoustic loudness (-19dB, 4%, linear RMS) and inter-frame pixel motion (motion:0.02) via a recursive-descent boolean S-expression AST (EditExpr), applies asymmetric frame margins & short-gap bridging (Margin), and exports non-destructive timelines to Final Cut Pro (.fcpxml), Premiere Pro, DaVinci Resolve, Shotcut, Kdenlive, CMX 3600 EDL, and FFmpeg filtergraphs.

03 // CRATE ARCHITECTURE (src/) EXACT RATIONAL TIMEBASE & AST ENGINE

src/expr.rs & audio.rs

Tokenizes and parses prefix S-expressions (and, or, not, audio:-19dB, motion:0.02) into an EditExpr AST with pre-computed linear thresholds ($10^{\text{dB}/20}$).

src/margin.rs & rational.rs

Uses exact num_rational::Ratio<i64> frame-rate math (30000/1001 NTSC, 24/1, 60/1) to expand pre/post speech margins and bridge short silence gaps.

src/timeline.rs & labels.rs

Resolves multi-layer priority labels (0 = Cut, 1 = Keep, 2..255 = Speed(f64)) into contiguous run-length encoded Chunk intervals and computes TimelineStats.

src/exports/ & render.rs

Serializes non-destructive timelines to FCPXML v1.10, Premiere v4, Resolve v5, Shotcut, Kdenlive, CMX 3600 EDL, CSV, JSON, and FFmpeg filter_complex graphs.
